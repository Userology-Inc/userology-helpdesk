Understanding the Results Section

General › Results and Reports | Updated: 2025-12-30

Once your study is published and participants begin completing sessions, Userology automatically organizes everything into the Results section once you click on the Conclude and Generate Results button (in the Recordings section).

This is where your research comes together.

Instead of shifting through raw recordings, transcripts, or notes, the Results section helps you:

  • Understand what participants said

  • Identify patterns and key findings

  • Turn observations into clear, shareable insights

Think of Results as your analysis workspace - whether you want a quick understanding or a polished report.

How Results Are Organized

The Results section is designed to support different ways of reviewing and working with research. To make analysis easier, Results are divided into multiple sections—each answering a different research need:

  • Qualitative Results – Deep, session-level insights from recordings and transcripts

  • Quantitative Results – Task metrics, ratings, and measurable outcomes

  • Discussion Summary – Patterns across participants

  • Report – A complete, structured research document

  • Ask AI – A conversational way to explore insights

  • Build Report – A way to generate personalized reports using AI

If you're looking to understand raw session data or metrics, start with:

  • Qualitative Results (covered in a separate article)

  • Quantitative Results (covered in a separate article)

This article focuses on the remaining Results tools—the ones that help you synthesize, interpret, and share insights.

Discussion Summary

The Discussion Summary gives you a structured, high-level view of participant responses across your study.

What this page shows

  • A matrix-style layout

  • Columns represent discussion topics or sections

  • Rows represent individual participants

  • AI-generated summaries capture key responses per topic

  • Direct links to session recordings for deeper context

How this complements Qualitative Results

While Qualitative Results focus on individual sessions, the Discussion Summary helps you:

  • Compare responses across participants

  • Identify themes without reviewing every transcript

  • Decide where deeper qualitative review is needed

This is often the first synthesis step after reviewing raw qualitative data.

Report

The Report section brings together qualitative insights, quantitative data, and AI synthesis into a single, structured research document.

What the report includes

  • Executive Summary

  • Study Objective & Methodology

  • Participant Background

  • Key Findings

    • Clearly written insights

    • Number of participants supporting each finding

    • Supporting participant quotes

  • Empathy Map

  • Quantitative Performance Data

  • Suggested Next Steps

What you can do here

  • Edit manually – Adjust wording, structure, or emphasis

  • Edit with AI – Improve clarity, tone, or depth

  • Download the report – Share it with stakeholders

The report is fully editable and reflects insights derived from both your Qualitative and Quantitative Results.

Ask AI

Ask AI lets you explore your study results by asking natural-language questions.

What Ask AI uses

  • Qualitative session data

  • Quantitative metrics

  • Patterns identified across the study

Example questions

  • "What is the highest-priority usability issue?"

  • "Which finding is supported by the most users?"

  • "What evidence backs this recommendation?"

Ask AI is especially useful after reviewing Quantitative or Qualitative Results and wanting quick, evidence-backed clarity.

Build Report

The Build Report feature helps you create custom AI-powered reports from your study data.

How it works

  • AI analyzes all qualitative and quantitative inputs

  • You specify what the report should focus on

  • The report is generated in a clean HTML format

  • You can refine and iterate through conversation

When to use Build Report

  • When different stakeholders need different views

  • When you want a focused report (e.g., usability issues only)

  • When you want multiple reports from the same study

You can create as many reports as needed—each tailored to a specific goal.

How to Choose the Right Results Option

Want raw participant feedback?→ Qualitative Results
Need metrics and task performance?→ Quantitative Results
Looking for patterns across participants?→ Discussion Summary
Need a complete research document?→ Report
Have specific questions?→ Ask AI
Creating a custom deliverable?→ Build Report
