Email Triggers in Userology

General › Settings and Admin | Updated: 2026-01-10

Userology keeps you informed at every important moment of your research study through automated email notifications. These emails are designed to ensure you never miss a critical update - whether it's getting started, tracking progress, or reviewing results.

This article explains the different types of email notifications you may receive, why each one is sent, and what it means for your study.

Account Creation Email

Why you receive this email: You receive this email after successfully creating your Userology account and gaining access to the dashboard. It confirms that your account is active and ready to use.

This email helps you:

  • Confirm that your signup was successful
  • Understand what you can do next in Userology
  • Begin your first research study with confidence

Research Completion Email

Why you receive this email: This email is sent when your research study has finished running and the final report has been generated.

It helps you:

  • Know that your study is complete
  • Access your synthesized insights and report
  • Share results with stakeholders or team members

First Participant Recruited (Publish for All – Automatic Recruitment)

Why you receive this email: You receive this email when the first participant has been successfully recruited after publishing your study to Userology's recruitment panel.

This email reassures you that:

  • Recruitment has started successfully
  • Your study is actively running
  • No action is required from you at this stage

First Response Received (Publish for All)

Why you receive this email: This email is sent when the first participant completes their research session and their response is processed and available in your dashboard.

It helps you:

  • Start reviewing real user feedback early
  • Access recordings, transcripts, and key moments
  • Understand that more insights will build as additional responses come in

First Response Received (Publish for One)

Why you receive this email: You receive this email when the single participant in a "Publish for One" study completes their session and the response is ready to review.

This email also informs you that:

  • The study is now paused after collecting one response
  • You can resume the study if you want to collect more responses

Response Limit Reached – Userology Recruitment

Why you receive this email: This email is sent after all participants recruited via Userology have completed their sessions and the study has stopped collecting responses.

It helps you:

  • Confirm that all planned responses have been collected
  • Review participant recordings
  • Conclude the study to begin generating the final synthesized report

Response Limit Reached – Manual Recruitment

Why you receive this email: You receive this email when all manually invited participants have completed their research sessions.

This email signals that:

  • Data collection is complete
  • You can review participant responses
  • You should conclude the study to generate the final report

How These Emails Help You

Userology's email notifications are designed to:

  • Keep you informed at every important milestone
  • Reduce the need to constantly monitor the dashboard
  • Guide you toward the right next action at the right time
  • Ensure your research runs smoothly from start to finish

Each email is sent only when there's a meaningful update - so you stay informed without being overwhelmed.
